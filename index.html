READING COMPREHENSION #1 (11 Puntos)

"Human Resources vs Conflict Resolution" — By Miranda Brook

[Párrafo 1] The human resources department, HR, frequently acts as a liaison between employees and managers, and among individual employees to facilitate consultations and avoid conflicts. It handles tasks such as verifying compliance with workplace policies through monitoring, executing audits and other supervisory reviews and handling disciplinary actions. Every business needs conflict resolution occasionally to uphold a victorious milestone of operation.

[Párrafo 2 - Employee Complaints] Headed by a human resources director, the HR department deals with allegations of unfair treatment, uninhabitable working conditions and conflict behaviors between co-workers. When an employee enters an internal complaint through the corporate grievance procedures, the department determines the appropriate course of action. It maintains records and keeps files of its results.

[Párrafo 3 - Labor Relations] If there's a dispute between employers and labor unions, HR commonly brings both networks to reach a consensus. Written policies determine the boundaries where negotiation starts and finishes. HR negotiates the terms of collective bargaining agreements related to benefits, wages and hours with union staff and looks after employees' civil rights. The department tries to maintain a friendly employer-employee environment and helps the parties reach a settlement.

[Párrafo 4 - Appraisals] Employee underperformance, harassment and illegal behavior also fall within the purview of human resources. If a direct supervisor tries to correct an employee's antics on the spot to no avail, someone in the HR department intervenes with the appraisal process. When an employee is incapable of working alongside others, human resources is tasked with terminating the rogue employee and filling her seat on the job.

[Párrafo 5 - Workplace Injury] Human resources prevents potential conflict if an employee sustains an on-the-job injury. Employees must first notify company officials. A staff specialist follows through directly with a workers' compensation claim. When the injury is the fault of the employer, for example, HR can forestall painful lawsuits by ensuring the worker receives medical attention and compensation for his injuries and lost wages.

[Párrafo 6 - 2016 Salary Information for Human Resources Managers] Human resources managers earned a median annual salary of $106,910 in 2016, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. On the low end, human resources managers earned a 25th percentile salary of $80,990, meaning 75 percent earned more than this amount. The 75th percentile salary is $145,220, meaning 25 percent earn more. In 2016, 136,100 people were employed in the U.S. as human resources managers.
